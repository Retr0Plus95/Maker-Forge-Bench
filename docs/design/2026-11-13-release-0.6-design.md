# Bench 0.6 "Where it goes" — design

Date: 2026-11-13. The owner said "keep going building". While building on their own board, their
questions have been "where do I connect C5, C9, C10" and "how do I connect tach A and B". Bench
should answer those in the board's own words. "Everywhere" moves to 0.7.

## 1. Hole names like your board's

Perfboards print a letter along one edge and a number along the other: the owner's electrokit
board has columns A, B, C… and rows 01–24.

- **Calibration.** On a lined-up photo, the Your board section asks for what's printed by three
  of the anchor's pins: pin 1, the far pin of its row (pin 14), and the pin across from pin 1
  (pin 28), for example L21, L8 and O21. They are typed, not tapped: the chip is already lined up.
- **The model.** Those three fix which way letters and numbers run in the anchor's frame, and
  where they start (`src/holenames.js`, pure and unit-tested).
  - Pin 1 to pin 14 runs along one axis, and pin 1 to pin 28 along the other, so their labels
    must change along one part each.
  - A wrong combination says what doesn't fit, for example "pin 1 and pin 14 should be 13 rows
    apart, these are 12".
- **Letters.** An optional "letters this board skips" field, for boards that leave out I, O or W.
  Columns after Z go AA, AB…
- **Where the names show:**
  - the part card: "On your board: + at L15, − at L14";
  - Check my board's findings and the suggested wires ("U2 pin 7 (L15) to C10 + (K13)");
  - the hover name on the photo;
  - the 3D view of your board, along its edges.
- **Stored:** in your board data (`myBoard.labels`), checked on read, and undoable.

## 2. Suggest a spot

For a part not placed yet, "Suggest a spot" on its card or in Your board.

- **The shapes:** the part's own shape from the project (its pins' offsets), turned four ways.
  Underside parts may sit on the legs they're soldered to (C5 across the chip's pins 7 and 8);
  top parts must take free holes.
- **The search:** slid around the holes near its connections (the placed legs on its lines).
  - Each spot is scored by the hole distance from each leg to the nearest placed leg on its line,
    plus a small cost for different lines on neighbouring holes.
  - Spots are refused if a leg lands on a taken hole (except an underside part on its own line's
    leg), or if a body overlaps (typical sizes).
- **Showing it:** the best three spots, drawn as dashed marks on the photo, with the reason
  ("next to U2 pin 7 and pin 8"). "Use this spot" places it (one undo step); "Another spot"
  moves on.
- **The search is pure** (`src/suggest.js`), unit-tested with the fume project: C5 lands on U2
  pins 7 and 8, and C9 on pin 20 and pin 22, as the drawing has them.

## 3. Fixes and file

- **Check my board:** an underside part soldered onto another part's legs (C5, C8, C9, Y1 on
  the chip) is not "two legs in one hole".
- **Save my board as a file / Open a board file:** your photos' line-ups, placements, wire shapes
  and hole names. The photos themselves stay in your folder.

## 4. Order

Hole names, then suggestions, then the fixes and the file. Tests first; push as each lands.
