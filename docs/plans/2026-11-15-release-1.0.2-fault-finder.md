# Bench 1.0.2 Fault finder: plan

Design: `docs/design/2026-11-15-release-1.0.2-design.md`. Built natively, no subagents (the owner
allows those only when they ask). Every task ends with its tests green and a commit.

## Global constraints
- Pages built with `el()` only, never innerHTML; Trusted Types `bench-svg` only; CSP unchanged.
- Every change to progress goes through `history.do` with a plain label.
- Storage keys stay `bench.*`; the progress field is `faults: { [symptomId]: { [checkId]: answer } }`,
  answer `{ said: 'yes' | 'no' }` or `{ reading: number }`.
- `bash check.sh` ends ALL CHECKS PASSED.

## Tasks
1. **`src/faults.js`, the symptoms** (`tests/unit/faults.test.js`): `railsOf(board)`,
   `wayIn(project)`, `pinNames(part)` (from the role: "pin 2 knob wiper"), `symptomsFor(project)`.
   The fume fixture gives the 12 symptoms listed in the design, in the parts list's order.
2. **`src/faults.js`, the checks**: `pathOf(board, ref, pins)`, `checksFor(symptomId, project)`,
   `judge(check, answer)`, `faultSummary(checks, answers)`, `faultQuestion(...)`, `FAULT_SYSTEM`,
   `cleanFaults(raw)`. The fume fixture's "Fan A always runs at full speed" includes the look at R12,
   the beep R12 pin 2 to U2 pin 4, the look at Q1 with its orientation, and volts on PWMBUS with no
   expectation; "U2 doesn't seem to run" expects pin 7 at 5 V ±0.5 and RST at 5 V.
3. **Progress**: `faults` in save, snapshot, progress file (export/import cleaned), versions, Start
   over; history labels.
4. **The section**: `#faults` in index.html, `renderFaults` in panel.js, app wiring (pick, answer,
   back, skip, fixed it, start again, show where, Ask Claude, Copy for Claude), palette entry, styles.
5. **3D probe marks**: `level: 'probe'` marks in board3d's setMarks (red +, black −), from the open
   check's legs.
6. **Browser tests** (`tests/browser/faults.test.js`): pick, answer each kind, a wrong answer and its
   fix, Back/Skip, reload keeps answers, undo, Ask Claude (mocked) and Copy, show where, 3D marks.
7. **Version 1.0.2, What's new, roadmap, design §7 As built**, full check, self-review, push.
